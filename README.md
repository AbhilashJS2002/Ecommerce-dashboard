# Ecommerce-dashboard
1.Project Title:
E-Commerce Dashboard


This interactive E-commerce Sales Dashboard tracks overall financial performance, regional sales, customer orders, payment preferences, and product profitability.

Headline KPIs:

Total Sales Amount: 438K (Sum of Amount)

Total Order Quantity: 6K items (Sum of Quantity)

Total Net Profit: 37K (Sum of Profit)

Regional Performance (Sales by State): Top-performing states by sales volume are led by Maharashtra, followed by Madhya Pradesh and Uttar Pradesh.

Product Performance:

Sales by Category: Clothing dominates volume at 63%, followed by Electronics (21%) and Furniture (17%).

Profitability by Sub-Category: Printers generated the highest profit (~8.5K), followed by Bookcases, Saree, Accessories, and Tables.

Financial & Monthly Dynamics:

Profit Trends: Profit peaked in January and November (~10K each month), but experienced notable profit dips/losses in May and July.

Payment Preferences: Cash on Delivery (COD) is the preferred payment mode at 44%, followed by UPI (21%), Credit Card (12%), Debit Card, and EMI (10%).
